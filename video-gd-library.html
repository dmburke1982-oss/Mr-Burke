Teacher Notes
Suggested lesson flow:
Before viewing: Have students open GMetrix and log in. Review the domain list on the board. Tell students to write down one thing they're unsure about for each domain.

During: Pause at 3:30 and 6:30 for quick pair-share checks. At 9:15 have students open their current portfolio project for comparison.

After viewing: Students complete one GMetrix domain practice. Log attempts in the Checkpoint Tracker.

48-min breakdown: 0:00–1:30 warm-up discussion (5 min) → play 1:30–6:30 (5 min) → GMetrix domain 1–2 practice (15 min) → play 6:30–end (6 min) → GMetrix domain 3–4 practice (15 min) → exit ticket (2 min).

Teacher Notes
How to use this audio course: This 63-minute course is designed as a listen-along reference, not a full-class video replacement. Suggested uses:

• Warm-up listen: Play Ch 1 (10 min) on Week 12 Day 1 as an orientation.
• Homework: Assign chapters to listen to before corresponding GMetrix practice.
• Exam week review: Play Ch 7 Exam Strategy the day before the ACE exam (8 min).
• Station rotation: Set up audio station with headphones — students listen to a chapter while others practice on GMetrix.

Download for offline use: Use the Download MP3 button to save to USB or local drive. Internet is required for streaming playback.
